Local SEO Guide & Checklist

Local SEO helps businesses improve their visibility when people search for products and services in a specific geographic area. This guide covers the fundamental areas businesses should review when building a local search strategy.

1. Google Business Profile

A complete and accurate Google Business Profile is an important part of a local search strategy. Businesses should use their real business information and select categories that accurately describe their services.

GBP Checklist

  • Use the correct real-world business name.
  • Select the most relevant primary category.
  • Add accurate business information.
  • Keep business hours current.
  • Add relevant services.
  • Upload useful, authentic business photos.
  • Monitor and respond to customer reviews.

2. Local Citations

Local citations are mentions of a business's name, address, phone number, or other business information on online directories and platforms.

Businesses should prioritize relevant and trustworthy directories rather than submitting to every directory available.

3. NAP Consistency

NAP refers to Name, Address, and Phone number. Business information should be accurate and consistent across important directories and other online properties.

NAP Audit Checklist

  • Business name is accurate.
  • Address is accurate where an address is publicly used.
  • Phone number is correct.
  • Website URL points to the correct domain.
  • Duplicate listings are identified and addressed.

4. Local On-Page SEO

Your website should clearly communicate what your business does, which locations it serves, and how customers can contact you.

Important areas include page titles, headings, service descriptions, internal links, location information, and useful content that matches the needs of local customers.

5. Customer Reviews

Reviews can influence how potential customers evaluate a business. Businesses should develop a legitimate process for requesting feedback from real customers and responding professionally to reviews.

6. Technical SEO

Local visibility also depends on a website being accessible to search engines and users. Important technical areas include crawlability, indexability, mobile usability, page speed, structured data where appropriate, and a clear internal linking structure.

7. Local SEO Audit

A useful local SEO audit should evaluate the business's website, Google Business Profile, citations, reviews, local landing pages, technical SEO, and competitive landscape.

Conclusion

Local SEO is not one single task. Strong local search visibility generally comes from maintaining accurate business information, providing useful website content, building a strong customer experience, and keeping important technical and local signals healthy over time.
