Google Business Profile Optimization Guide

A Google Business Profile helps eligible businesses manage how their business information appears across Google Search and Maps. Keeping the profile accurate, complete, and useful can make it easier for customers to discover and evaluate a business.

1. Use Accurate Business Information

Start with accurate information about the real-world business. Business name, address or service-area information, phone number, website, and operating hours should accurately represent the business.

Business Information Checklist

  • Use the real business name.
  • Provide accurate contact information.
  • Keep operating hours updated.
  • Use the correct website URL.
  • Keep special hours updated when necessary.

2. Choose the Most Relevant Category

Categories help describe what a business does. Choose categories that accurately represent the business rather than selecting categories simply because they contain desirable keywords.

3. Add Relevant Services

Where the available profile features support them, businesses can provide information about the services they offer. Service information should accurately describe the real services provided.

4. Add Authentic Photos

Useful, authentic photographs can help potential customers understand the business. Depending on the business type, relevant images may include the exterior, interior, team, products, completed work, or other genuine aspects of the business.

5. Manage Customer Reviews

Businesses should encourage genuine customers to share honest feedback and should respond professionally to reviews. Do not manufacture reviews or offer prohibited incentives in exchange for positive reviews.

6. Keep the Profile Updated

Business information can change over time. Regularly review hours, services, contact information, photos, and other profile details so customers are not presented with outdated information.

7. Connect the Profile With Your Website

Your website should clearly describe the business and its services. Important local pages should provide useful information rather than simply repeating keywords.

8. Monitor Local Search Performance

A local SEO strategy should be evaluated over time. Review changes in search visibility, customer actions, website traffic, calls, direction requests, and other relevant business outcomes.

Conclusion

Google Business Profile optimization is not about adding as many keywords as possible. The strongest approach is to maintain accurate business information, select appropriate categories, provide useful details, use authentic content, and keep the profile current.
